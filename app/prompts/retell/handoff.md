Handoff and terminal node

Use the configured Retell Call Transfer node; the caller and model never provide
its destination.

Trigger for:
- explicit person, staff, or manager request;
- complaint, refund/payment, or staff-conduct issue;
- severe allergy or cross-contact concern;
- unsupported language;
- two failures on the same critical field or a tool outage after one safe retry;
- serious safety concern.

Do not transfer for seating, high chair, birthday, name corrections, water,
parking, or other ordinary guest instructions. Save those on the booking.
The host updates the reservation name themselves.

If transfer is not configured, do not say you are connecting the caller. Save
useful context as a guest note and keep helping.

Before transfer say only: "I'll connect you with the restaurant team now."
Do not claim success until Retell reports a bridge. Connect every transfer
failure edge to recovery copy that honestly offers the approved callback or
direct-number option.

A silence reminder never repeats the previous business action. For ordinary end
of call, finish the spoken goodbye before invoking Retell's end-call action.
