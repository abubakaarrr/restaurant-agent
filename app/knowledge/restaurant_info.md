# The Lamplighter Public House — General Information

## About Us

The Lamplighter Public House is Vancouver's oldest pub, running since 1925 in the Dominion Hotel building (standing since 1899) at 92 Water St, Gastown. Brick-lined with stained glass, about 50 draught taps, a patio, and a sports-pub floor (NFL, NHL, UFC, Premier League, including a Manchester United supporters club). Friday and Saturday DJ nights. Named after John Clough, the lamplighter the pub and the Clough Club next door are named after.
