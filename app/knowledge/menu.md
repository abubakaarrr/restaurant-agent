# Harbor & Hearth Kitchen — Legacy Offline Menu Summary

The complete menu is stored as structured synthetic records in
`db/fixtures/harbor_and_hearth.v1.json` and seeded into `menu_items`. Runtime
tools return those canonical records, including ingredients, allergens,
dietary tags, service periods, availability, prices, modifiers, substitutions,
removals, incompatibilities, alternatives, and cross-contact guidance.

## Allergy safety

The kitchen uses shared equipment and preparation areas. Never promise zero
cross-contact or call an item allergen-free unless a current canonical record
explicitly supports that statement. Severe allergy questions require the
configured kitchen/staff route or an honest callback/message fallback.
